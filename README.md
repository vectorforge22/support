## What does sponsorship support?

I work across a fairly broad range of technical and creative projects.

Support may help cover:

- Open-source software development and maintenance
- AI and agentic systems research
- Compute and development infrastructure
- Hosting, domains, APIs, and online services
- Hardware for development, testing, and experimentation
- Documentation and technical writing
- Books, frameworks, and other publications
- Data, research materials, and software tools
- Experimental projects that may not have an immediate commercial return

Not every experiment needs to become a product.

Part of the value of independent work is being able to investigate ideas because they are interesting, useful, or worth testing.

My work changes over time, but generally includes projects involving:

### Open-source software

Tools and projects that I publish publicly for others to use, study, modify, or build upon.

Experiments involving local AI, autonomous and semi-autonomous agents, developer tooling, evaluation frameworks, memory systems, and human-AI workflows.

Trying to turn messy practical problems into reusable concepts, methodologies, benchmarks, and frameworks.

Documentation, essays, guides, books, and other material intended to make complicated ideas easier to understand and use.

## Transparency

Sponsorship supports my broader independent work rather than purchasing a guaranteed feature, deliverable, or amount of development time unless we have explicitly agreed otherwise.

I may occasionally describe what I'm currently working on or what sponsorship is helping make possible, but I don't want this to become a system where useful public work is artificially locked behind supporter tiers.

When practical, I prefer to publish things openly.
